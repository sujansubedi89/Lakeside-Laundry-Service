When you have a day trip, trek, or bus connection on the schedule, finding time to carry laundry across town can be inconvenient. Lakeside Laundry Services offers free delivery to hotels, hostels, and homestays along Lakeside Road, Streets 1 to 17, Baidam, and the Jarebar lakeside perimeter. Send your accommodation and preferred handoff time to arrange the service. Pickup and delivery timing still needs to be agreed in WhatsApp so both handoffs fit your plans.

Make a useful pickup request

A few details help us check the request without a long back-and-forth. Include the name of the hotel, hostel, or other meeting point; a street or map pin if the name is not clear; how many bags or items you have; the type of service; and the time window that works for you. If reception should receive the laundry, tell us and confirm the handoff directly with the accommodation. Do not send room access codes or other sensitive information through a public form.

Confirm both handoffs

In WhatsApp, agree on where and when to hand over the laundry. Before collection, confirm what is included, any special instructions, and when you should expect it back. The return timing depends on the service and current availability. If your travel plan has a firm departure, say so at the start and wait for an explicit confirmation before arranging your schedule. For a time-sensitive load, read about express laundry availability.

Ask what pickup costs

No collection fee, delivery charge, per-kilogram rate, or item rate has been provided. Request one clear total for your order, and ask whether pickup or return is a separate charge. Prices may depend on the service, items, quantity, or requested turnaround; we will not show a made-up rate here. Use the prices page to see what details to include when requesting a quote.

Suitable for visitors and local stays

This option can help tourists who would rather stay near their accommodation, trekkers returning with clothes or equipment, and guests with limited time between check-out and their next stop. We also take enquiries from hotels and hostels about guest laundry or repeat arrangements. Larger or regular requests should include an approximate load and schedule; see our notes for hotel and hostel laundry enquiries. For bulky outdoor items, ask separately about cleaning trekking gear, because drying can take longer than ordinary clothes.
