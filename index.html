Quick answers

Laundry questions

Can you collect laundry from my hotel or hostel?

Free delivery covers stays along Lakeside Road, Streets 1 to 17, Baidam, and the Jarebar lakeside perimeter. Send your accommodation name to arrange the handoff.

Which express turnaround options are available?

One-hour service is for urgent drop-offs during morning or early-evening windows. Three-hour express and same-day evening return are also offered. Confirm the right option for your load before handover.

How much does laundry cost?

Prices are not published. See the laundry prices page and message us for a quote.

Can you clean trekking equipment?

Ask about down jackets, sleeping bags, shoes, and bags. Tell us the item and its care-label instructions; drying time varies by item and conditions.
