For a tight travel schedule

Express Laundry in Pokhara

Short on clean clothes before your next bus, trek, or check-out? Ask whether express laundry and pickup are available for your load today. We confirm timing before you book.

Ask about express pickupAsk for a price

Travel plans rarely leave much spare time for laundry. If you are changing accommodation, preparing for a trek, or heading to another town, message Lakeside Laundry Services with your location and the time you need your clothes back. Three express choices are offered: one-hour drop-off during morning or early-evening windows, three-hour express, and same-day evening return for morning drop-offs. The one-hour option is specifically for drop-offs at the shop; pickup and hotel return need to be arranged separately. Confirm your preferred service and handoff before planning around a deadline.

Choose the turnaround that fits

For a one-hour request, bring the urgent load to the shop during a morning or early-evening window. The exact window is confirmed directly, so message first rather than making an unnecessary trip. Three-hour express covers rapid washing, mechanical tumbling, and folding; tell us the size of the load and when you can drop it off. Same-day service is for laundry dropped off in the morning and scheduled to be ready by evening, either for pickup or hotel delivery. Give us your hotel name when requesting delivery. If you are already checked into accommodation, ask whether pickup is available and which turnaround suits your load.

Pickup from your accommodation

If collecting from your hotel or hostel would save you a walk, include its name, street, and a contact point such as reception. We will confirm whether that address is within the pickup area and agree a handoff time with you. Pickup and return are arranged directly in WhatsApp. Read more about the Lakeside laundry pickup and delivery process, including what details to send and how to confirm a handoff.

What it costs

No per-kilogram rate or express surcharge was supplied for publication. Ask for the complete quote before confirming, and check whether it covers the selected service, any express surcharge, and pickup or delivery. The laundry prices page explains what to include in a quote request. The final price and agreed return time should be clear in the chat before collection.

How to request an express order

  1. Send your hotel or location and preferred pickup time.
  2. Describe the load and say when you need it returned.
  3. Ask whether the requested turnaround is available and request the full price.
  4. Confirm the handoff and return details in writing before arranging your day.

For a hotel or hostel handling regular guest laundry, the hotel and hostel laundry page has separate information about larger or repeat requests. For everyday loads without a deadline, compare wash and fold in Pokhara.

Frequently asked questions

When can I use the one-hour option?

For urgent loads dropped off in the morning or early evening. Ask us to confirm the window before setting out; the one-hour option does not imply a one-hour pickup service.

What does three-hour express include?

Rapid washing, mechanical tumbling, and folding. Share the load details and confirm it is suitable before handing it over.

Can I have same-day delivery to a hotel?

Morning drop-offs can be scheduled for evening pickup or hotel delivery. Provide the accommodation and confirm the handoff when you order.
