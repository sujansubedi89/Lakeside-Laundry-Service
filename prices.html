Get a quote you can plan around

Before arranging collection, ask us to confirm the charge for your exact service and load. A quote is most useful when it separates the laundry price from any express, pickup, or delivery charge. If you have several different items, list them individually so special gear does not get confused with a standard clothing load.

Details to send with your request

  • The service you want, such as wash and fold, ironing, or gear cleaning.
  • An approximate weight, bag count, or item list.
  • Your hotel, hostel, or pickup location.
  • Your preferred pickup time and return deadline.
  • Any care-label information or special item instructions.

For a time-sensitive order, first read how to check express availability. For regular guest loads or larger batches, ask about hotel and hostel partner rates. The trekking gear page explains why down-filled items may need additional drying time.

Confirm before pickup

Wait for our reply and make sure you understand the total, what service is included, whether collection and return are covered, and the expected timing. Rates have not been published because they were not provided; no charge should be inferred from this page.
