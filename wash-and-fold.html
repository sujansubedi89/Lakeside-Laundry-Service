After several days on the road, it is useful to have ordinary clothes ready to wear again without spending your own travel time washing them. Lakeside Laundry Services offers wash and fold requests in Lakeside, Pokhara. Send us an estimate of the load and the place you are staying; we can tell you whether pickup and the requested turnaround are available. Confirm what is included before the order is collected so the result fits your plans.

What to tell us about the load

A rough weight or number of bags is a helpful start. Mention whether the load is mostly everyday shirts, trousers, socks, or other clothing, and point out anything that should be treated separately. If a garment has a care label with special instructions, share those instructions before pickup. Let us know about delicate pieces, strong stains, or anything you want left out of the wash. Do not put valuable items in a laundry bag, and check pockets before the handoff.

Timing depends on today's workload

No standard return time has been confirmed for wash and fold. Tell us when you would like to hand over the load and when you need it back. We will confirm the available turnaround before you rely on it. If you are changing accommodation or leaving Pokhara, say so at the start. Do not assume that a same-day return is possible; for a deadline, ask whether express laundry is available for your specific load.

Ask how the price is calculated

No per-kilogram rate, minimum load, or pickup fee was supplied. Ask us for a quote and confirm whether it is based on weight, pieces, or another measure. Also check whether delivery is included and whether any requested ironing is priced separately. If you want garments pressed as well, list the pieces and ask for an itemized quote. The laundry price page is transparent about which rates are still awaiting confirmation.

Pickup from your stay

You can ask us to collect from a hotel, hostel, or another location in Lakeside. Share the exact address or accommodation name and a preferred handoff time. We will confirm whether the location is served before agreeing the collection. Read more about the pickup and return arrangements. Guests and accommodation teams with repeat or larger loads can also review the hotel and hostel laundry information.

How to place a wash-and-fold request

  1. Estimate your load and share your accommodation.
  2. Describe any care instructions or items to keep separate.
  3. Give your preferred pickup and return times.
  4. Ask for the full quote, including any pickup or delivery charge.
  5. Confirm the price and timing in WhatsApp before arranging collection.
