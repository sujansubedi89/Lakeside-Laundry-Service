Accommodation teams often need a clear way to ask about laundry without making promises to guests before the details are known. Lakeside Laundry Services takes enquiries from hotels and hostels in Pokhara about guest loads, larger batches, and repeat service. The right arrangement depends on your location, the size and frequency of requests, and what can be accommodated at the time. Contact us to discuss the practical details before setting expectations with guests.

Start with your property's needs

Tell us the property name and exact location, who will coordinate handoffs, and an estimate of the number of bags, items, or kilograms in a typical request. If guest demand varies, say whether you expect occasional individual loads or predictable batches. Let us know how often you are considering service and the days or time windows that could work. These details help us respond with a useful availability check rather than a generic promise.

Discuss repeat service and partner rates

Partner rates are not listed because none were provided. Contact us with your expected volume and schedule to discuss a quote for your situation. Ask whether pricing is calculated by weight, item, or batch; whether collection and return are included; and how any unusually large or delicate items are handled. Confirm any agreed rate and service details directly in writing. Our public price page shows the information that is currently available and explains how to request a quote.

Set realistic guest expectations

No standard hotel turnaround or collection timetable has been confirmed. Discuss each requested return time with us before relaying it to a guest. If a guest has a departure deadline, share it immediately and ask for an explicit confirmation. For individual time-sensitive loads, refer guests to our page about checking express laundry availability. Pickup and delivery must also be confirmed for the property's address; read the pickup and delivery details before setting a collection point.

A practical handoff routine

Choose one staff contact for each order, agree where bags will be left, and identify any labels or guest references needed to return items to the right person. Keep special instructions with the individual load and ask us to confirm that we can accommodate them. Do not send guest personal details that are not needed to identify the laundry. The business address, phone, and opening hours appear on the contact page; use WhatsApp to coordinate a specific arrangement.
