Trekking gear collects dust, sweat, and trail residue, but insulated equipment is not the same as an ordinary clothing load. Lakeside Laundry Services lists cleaning for down jackets, sleeping bags, shoes, and bags. Before arranging pickup, tell us exactly what the item is, what it is made of if you know, and what its care label says. We can discuss whether it is suitable for the service and what timing to expect. This page does not promise a particular cleaning method or result for every material.

Down jackets and sleeping bags need time

Down jackets receive specialist loft care, while sleeping bags can be deep cleaned and fluffed to support their insulation. Insulated items can take longer to dry than everyday clothes. Drying time varies with the item's fill and construction, its care instructions, the weather, and available drying conditions. A damp or incompletely dried item should not be treated as ready for a trek. If you have a departure date, tell us before pickup and ask whether there is enough time for cleaning and drying. We cannot promise a fixed drying duration without checking the item and current conditions.

Include the care information

When you message, mention the brand or item type if known, point out stains or damaged areas, and share the care-label instructions. A clear photo of the label can help explain its washing guidance; ask us first if you are unsure how to send it. Also note whether the gear has detachable liners or removable insoles. Be direct about areas that need attention and any part you do not want treated. We will discuss whether the request can be accommodated before you leave the item.

Shoes and bags

Hiking shoes and boots can receive a stain wash and odour scrub. Materials and construction vary, so share the shoe type and any care instructions before cleaning. Trekking bags are also accepted; tell us whether a bag has a frame, waterproof coating, leather panels, or detachable pieces. These details help us discuss the request and quote; they do not guarantee a particular treatment. How to request gear cleaning

  1. Send the item names and your hotel or location.

  5. Confirm those details with us before handing over the gear.
